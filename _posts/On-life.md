Why does God expects rituals, worships when living itself is greatest act of penance, there is no agony greater than living. Is flogging oneself any different than living? It's merely a false extension of suffering, for no torments surmount to ones that life hands you with. The sufferings of life outweigh its own birth by tenth fold degrees, for someone who has made it through life without harming oneself has performed the greatest penance, why must god ask for worship and more penance then? Hell is not any worse than living, just an intense imitation of all the sufferings and agony of this so called 'life'. Comparing a life of an ascetic and a life of someone who made it through is like comparing 1+ and 1, any penance barely or doesn't make any difference. Death, sickness, pain, hardships, agony and all kinds of illness in this world, are they any less than hell? Then there are optimists trying to create a false utopia of their own, because they can't confront the sufferings of life so they disguise their fear of death, of sufferings and of all kinds of agonies of something they think is morally greater or superior to sadness. But what is the use of all that quotations when they cry like a little girl when anything bad happens to them? The person who commits suicide is not a pessimist rather often a failed optimist, pessimists have long confronted the sufferings of life and all the lies and made peace with it. I don't believe though, that sadness and melancholy are an excuse for laziness, ambition with all its agonies confronted always finds itself at a much higher place than ambition that always demands success with laziness. Sadness and melancholy shall never be a substitute for hard work, if life is misery, then the clump of decaying cells that we are born to is a medium of rebellion, the consciousness, while most pessimists would argue it's a great tragedy, I'm very grateful for consciousness because there's always a possibility that I can rebel by rearranging these clump of decomposing cells according to my will, that I can think. For to think is a greatest gift, because thoughts can shape the world, and to think is an act of defiance against the objective world. There's a great joy in thinking and understanding because it reveals some of the joys that men can have whilst they are still alive, they manifest curiosity and innocence of a child who knows his terrible fate and still loves it yet humbly rejects it. If I am going to lose anyway? Then there is no fear of losing, only a diminished possibility of winning. What are pleasures to a man who craves suffering? Who rather finds comfort and a home in it. A home in unfamiliarity, the world has already alienated him so much that even the unfamiliar things feel like a familiar place, even sewer drains feel like a safe place and even the dangerous alleys feel safe enough.
